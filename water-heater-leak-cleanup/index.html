A standard tank water heater holds 40 to 50 gallons in most homes. When the steel tank corrodes through, the contents can drain onto the floor in a matter of minutes, and the supply line keeps refilling the tank until the cold water is closed.

A water heater leak is a distinct kind of loss because the water is clean but warm, the source is in a utility closet, garage or attic, and it often runs unnoticed for a long time.

Water heater emergency: what to do first

1. Close the cold water supply valve above the heater. The handle turns clockwise to close.

2. Turn off the power at the breaker for an electric heater, or turn the gas control to off for a gas heater.

3. If water is near the electrical panel or the heater wiring, stay out and call the utility or 911.

4. Open a hot water tap elsewhere in the house to relieve pressure.

5. Photograph the heater, its label and the floor before anything is moved.

After-hours failures are common because heaters give out under steady use, so the dispatch line is open overnight. Crews go out the same day whenever one is available.

Why water heaters fail

Most tank failures come from internal corrosion after years of service, from sediment that bakes onto the tank bottom, or from a relief valve that discharges and is left open. Slow leaks start at a fitting, a drain valve, a relief valve discharge pipe or the connection to the supply lines.

A heater in an attic or a second-floor closet makes the damage worse because water passes through the ceiling below.

Warning signs include rust-colored streaks on the tank base, a damp spot on the floor under the heater, a popping or rumbling sound and water that runs lukewarm. If you see any of these on a heater that is more than ten years old, consider replacing it before it fails on its own schedule.

How a crew cleans up after a water heater leak

Extraction comes first. The crew pulls standing water from the closet, hallway or garage floor and checks the walls, flooring and anything stored nearby. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where the water traveled under flooring and into the base of walls. Wet drywall at the base is cut out at a clean line, and wet insulation and carpet pad are removed.

Drying equipment then runs in the affected space. In a small closet, a few air movers and one dehumidifier may be enough. Moisture readings are checked daily and compared with unaffected areas. After the structure is dry, the baseboard, drywall and flooring are repaired.

Water heater leaks in attics and upper floors

A leaking heater above a ceiling means ceiling damage below it. If you see a stain on the ceiling below a utility closet, see our ceiling water damage repair page for what happens to drywall and insulation. Insulation soaked in an attic dries slowly and is often replaced.

Repair or replace

A plumber replaces the water heater or repairs a fitting. A restoration crew does not replace the unit. Ask the plumber whether a drain pan with a drain line, a leak sensor or an expansion tank would limit the damage if the unit leaks again.

What affects the cost of a water heater leak cleanup

Questions Oxford homeowners ask

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Oxford?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered overnight and on weekends. Shut off the cold water supply to the heater first, then call, and dispatch lines up a crew. Ask about a same-day visit, which depends on crew availability, and we do not promise an arrival time.

Where is the cold water shut-off for the heater?

On the pipe that enters the top of the tank, usually with a handle or a round knob. If you cannot find it, close the main valve for the house and call.

Is hot water dangerous in a leak?

The water in a tank can be hot enough to scald. Do not touch standing water near the heater until it has cooled.

Does insurance cover a water heater leak?

The resulting water damage from a sudden failure is often covered, but the heater itself usually is not, and long-term leaks often are not. Ask your insurer.

Can I replace the heater before the cleanup?

The plumber can replace it first, but the leak area should still be extracted and dried so the closet floor and walls are not left wet.

Ask about the industry standard

Professional water damage work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. When you compare companies for water heater leak cleanup work, ask whether the crew works to it and how moisture readings will be recorded.
