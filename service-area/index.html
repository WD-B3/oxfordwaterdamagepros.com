Water Damage Service Area: Oxford and Taylor, MS

Dispatch runs from Oxford across Lafayette County. Here is where crews go and how to tell what is reachable.

☎ Call (662) 757-1011

Oxford, MS 38655

Oxford is the home base and the busiest part of the service area. Crews work across the city, from the homes around the courthouse Square and the neighborhoods near the University of Mississippi campus to Freedmen Town and the newer subdivisions along Highway 6 and Highway 7. Highway 6, also US 278, is the main east and west route through town. Highway 7 runs north and south.

Both matter because they decide how quickly a crew reaches a job on a rainy night when side streets are slow.

The city provides water and sewer service through Oxford Utilities, and the City of Oxford Building Department at 107 Courthouse Square handles permits and inspections. When you call, mention the street and the nearest crossroad so dispatch can plan the route. If you are asking about water damage restoration in Oxford, the homepage explains the full process.

Taylor, MS 38673

Taylor is a small Lafayette County town southwest of the city, a short drive from Oxford. It has its own water association, its own town hall and its own floodplain rules, so a job there involves different phone numbers than a job in Oxford. The full write-up is on the Taylor water damage restoration page, including the nearby communities of Abbeville and Paris.

Calling from outside these areas

If you are in another part of Lafayette County or a nearby town, call the line and ask. Dispatch will tell you honestly whether a crew can reach you the same day. We do not promise arrival times, because storms, road closures and call volume change what is possible.

What to have ready when you call

- The street address and the nearest cross street

- Where the water started and whether it is still running

- Whether the water looks clean, gray or contaminated

- The rooms and materials that are wet, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or ceilings

- Whether you have already turned off the main water valve or the power to the area

Having those five answers ready lets the dispatcher give you safe next steps and plan the right equipment before anyone arrives.
