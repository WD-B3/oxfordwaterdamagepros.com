Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage in Oxford, MS

A leak under a concrete slab hides until floors warm, bills climb or water shows. Find out what to do and how cleanup works.

When water appears on a floor and no fixture is running, the source may be a pipe under the concrete. Emergency slab leak water damage cleanup in Oxford, MS begins with confirming the leak and closing the main valve. Check your water meter with every fixture off. If it keeps moving, a line is leaking, and a slab leak is one place the water could be going.

A slab leak is a leak in a pipe that runs under or through a concrete foundation slab. The water comes up through cracks, along the slab edge or into flooring on top of it.

Because the pipe is buried, the leak can run for weeks before it shows, and the first sign is often a warm spot on the floor, a high water bill or the sound of running water when nothing is on.

Do you have a slab leak?

Whether your home sits on a slab, a crawl space or a basement is visible from outside and on your property record. This page applies to slab-on-grade homes. In a home with a crawl space, the same leak shows up in the crawl space and is covered on that page.

Signs of a slab leak include:

- A warm or hot spot on the floor

- Water pooling at a wall base or under flooring

- Cracked or lifted floor tile

- A water bill that climbs with no change in use

- The sound of running water with all fixtures off

- A meter that keeps moving with every fixture closed

What to do about a possible slab leak

1. Check the meter with all fixtures and appliances off.

2. Close the main valve and see whether the meter stops. If it does, the leak is after the meter.

3. Call a plumber to locate and repair the leak, and call our 24/7 line for water on floors.

4. Photograph wet flooring, cracked tile and any damp walls before cleanup.

5. Keep water use low until the plumber arrives.

Plumbing leaks do not wait for business hours, and the dispatch line is open overnight. Same-day visits are offered when a crew is free.

Water on the floor with no visible source

Some people describe this as a spot that keeps coming back or a floor that is always damp. A leak under a slab saturates the soil and the slab and can push moisture up into floor coverings. Wood flooring and laminate cup or buckle. Tile may lift or crack. Carpet gets damp and smells musty from underneath.

This is the one source where the first question is not cleanup. A plumber has to find the line, using acoustic listening devices or a thermal camera, and either repair it through a small opening in the slab or reroute the pipe overhead. The restoration side begins once the leak is controlled.

How a crew handles slab leak damage

The crew extracts any standing water and checks floor coverings with moisture meters. Carpet and pad often come up. Laminate and engineered wood that have swollen are removed. Tile may be left if the substrate is sound. Concrete holds water and releases it slowly, so drying a slab takes longer than drying a wall. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings stabilize.

Do not install new flooring over a damp slab. A moisture test on the concrete, and not a guess, decides when it is dry enough for finishes.

What affects the cost of slab leak cleanup

COST

How long the leak ran :: Weeks of leaking soaks a slab and everything over it.

Flooring type :: Carpet and laminate are cheaper to replace than hardwood or large-format tile.

Area affected :: A leak under one room is a smaller job than one under a hallway and bedrooms.

Plumbing repair :: Locating and repairing the line is a plumbing cost, separate from the cleanup.

Drying days :: Concrete dries slowly and needs time and monitoring.

You will hear the scope and price before work begins. Insurance coverage for slab leaks varies, and some policies exclude damage from leaks that are gradual or inside the foundation, so ask your insurer.

Questions Oxford homeowners ask

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Oxford?

Yes, when water is on a floor. The dispatch line is answered 24/7, and dispatch will say whether a crew can get there the same day. A plumber has to locate the leak itself.

How do I tell if the leak is under the slab?

A meter that moves with everything off, warm floor spots and wet flooring with no visible source are strong signs. A plumber confirms it.

Will you repair the pipe?

No. A plumber repairs or reroutes the pipe. We extract and dry, and repair the finishes.

Is a slab leak covered by insurance?

It varies. Some policies cover resulting water damage but not the cost of reaching the pipe. Ask your insurer.

Can I wait to see if the spot dries?

A leak under concrete does not stop on its own. Check the meter, and call a plumber quickly.

Ask about the industry standard

Professional water damage work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. When you compare companies for slab leak water damage work, ask whether the crew works to it and how moisture readings will be recorded.
