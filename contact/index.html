Call the 24/7 dispatch line

Dial (662) 757-1011 and describe what happened. A dispatcher will ask where the water started, whether it is still running and what is wet. You will get safe next steps on the same call, and a crew will be arranged based on the job and what is available.

Email for non-emergencies

Use email for billing questions, documentation requests or follow-ups on a finished job. Email is not monitored the way the phone is, so never use it for an active leak.

Before you call

Have the street address, the source of the water and the rooms affected ready. If you can safely take photos first, do so, because they help with the insurance claim later. If water is touching electrical outlets or the panel, call the utility or 911 before you call us.
