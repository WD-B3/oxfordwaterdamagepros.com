About Avent Acres

Avent Acres is a residential area in Oxford made up of about 100 homes along Avent and Combs streets and Williams, Sisk and Chandler avenues. The Oxford Eagle reported in 2018 that the houses date from after World War II, when the Avent family subdivided part of its land. The lots are small and the homes are modest.

The same report noted that many of the houses are still standing in their original form, likely because they serve as student and rental housing.

Why a leak matters in a small house

A compact floor plan has fewer rooms between a leak and everything else. Water that starts at a washing machine hose or a water heater can reach a hallway, a bedroom and a kitchen in one evening. That is a point about floor plans and not a claim about how often leaks happen here. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status has not been verified for Avent Acres.

Common water damage jobs in a rental or family home

Most calls from a neighborhood like this start inside the house. A supply line under a sink gives way. A water heater tank starts to weep. A dishwasher or washer hose splits behind a machine nobody has moved in months. Each of these is a clean-water event when it is caught early, and each can soak flooring and the bottom of a wall.

- Appliance hose or supply-line leaks, covered under appliance leak water damage

- Tank leaks, covered under water heater leak cleanup

- Soaked floors, covered under carpet water damage restoration

- Pipe failures, covered under burst pipe water damage

If you rent your home

A tenant and a landlord both have a stake in a leak, so the first hour is about records. Close the valve if you can reach it safely, photograph the water and what it touched, and report the leak to the landlord or property manager in writing, with the date. Move your own belongings out of the wet area.

The structure belongs to the owner, and the owner decides on repairs, while your belongings may be covered by a renters policy if you have one. Ask your insurer what applies.

Safety first

Stay out of standing water if it may be near outlets or the panel. Do not run ordinary fans or a household vacuum to deal with water from a drain or a sewer line. Shut off the water only if the valve is safe to reach. If something looks dangerous, call the utility or 911 before you call us.

What the work looks like

A crew removes standing water, measures moisture in the floor and walls and then dries the affected area with air movers and dehumidifiers. Wet carpet pad and soft drywall are often cut out so the framing can dry. The water extraction page describes the first step, and the structural drying page explains the rest. Moisture readings and photos can be shared with your insurer or landlord.

Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the loss.

Common questions

Is Avent Acres in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status has not been verified. Flood zones are mapped by property, so check your address on the City of Oxford floodplain lookup or with the city engineering office.

Who pays for a leak in a rental house?

It depends on the lease, the cause and the insurance each party holds. Report the leak to the landlord in writing and ask your insurer about contents coverage.

Can a washing machine hose leak soak the whole house?

It can spread far in a small floor plan if it runs for hours. Close the valve, remove standing water and have the floor and walls checked with a moisture meter.
