Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Oxford, MS

Carpet and pad hold water like a sponge. Decide what can be dried in place and what has to come out.

☎ Call (662) 757-1011

Carpet water damage restoration in Oxford, MS begins with a simple question: what kind of water was it, and how long has it been there? Carpet is a surface layer over a foam or fiber pad, and the pad is where the water collects.

A carpet that feels damp on top is usually soaked underneath, and the decisions about what to keep depend on how deep the water is and how clean it was.

A water category is a way of classifying the water source. Category 1 comes from a clean source such as a supply line. Category 2, sometimes called gray water, comes from sources such as a washing machine or dishwasher drain. Category 3 includes sewage and floodwater. Carpet that contacted Category 3 water is generally discarded.

Carpet emergency: the first steps

1. Stop the water source.

2. Remove standing water with towels or a wet vacuum if it is shallow and the area is dry of electrical hazards.

3. Lift furniture off the carpet and place foil or plastic under any legs that must stay.

4. Do not walk on wet carpet more than necessary.

5. Photograph the damage and call for extraction.

Wet carpet begins to smell within a day or two in warm weather. The dispatch line is open 24/7, and ask about a same-day visit, which depends on crew availability.

Why the pad decides the outcome

Carpet pad is a sponge. It soaks up water and releases it slowly, and it often cannot be cleaned. When the water was clean and the pad is saturated, many crews remove and replace it, because replacing pad costs far less than risking mold and odor. The carpet may be saved if it is lifted, supported on a rack or air-gapped and dried quickly.

Callers sometimes pulled up a corner of carpet and found staining and dampness beneath. That means the pad and subfloor were wet, and extraction and drying are needed.

Floating, drying in place and replacing

Floating means lifting the carpet from the tack strip, removing the pad and drying both the carpet and the subfloor. It works best with clean water caught within a day or two. Drying in place leaves the carpet attached and uses extraction and air movers, which suits shallow, clean-water events on a short time scale.

Replacement is the right call when the water was contaminated, the carpet has delaminated or the water sat too long.

Carpet backing can separate from the carpet when it stays wet, and wrinkles and rippling show it. A carpet that has lost its backing cannot be stretched back.

What the crew does

The crew extracts water with a weighted wand, then checks the pad and subfloor with moisture meters. The pad is removed if needed, and the carpet is floated or dried in place. Air movers blow air across the carpet and under it, and dehumidifiers keep the humidity low. The crew checks readings every day until they are dry.

Carpet over a wet slab

Carpet over concrete picks up moisture from a slab leak and smells musty even without a visible puddle. If damp carpet keeps returning to damp after drying, check the slab. The slab leak page explains how a leak under concrete shows up.

Questions Oxford homeowners ask

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and the carpet was dried within a day or two. The pad usually has to be replaced.

How do I know if the pad is wet?

Press on the carpet. If water squeezes up or the area feels cold and spongy, the pad is wet. A moisture meter confirms it.

Should I rent a carpet cleaner?

A household cleaner removes some water but not what is in the pad and subfloor. For more than a small spill, call a crew.

How long does carpet take to dry?

A few days with air movers and dehumidifiers when the water was clean and conditions are good.

Will the carpet smell?

Wet carpet can develop odor within a day or two. Fast extraction and drying prevent it.

Ask about the industry standard

Professional water damage work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. When you compare companies for carpet water damage work, ask whether the crew works to it and how moisture readings will be recorded.
