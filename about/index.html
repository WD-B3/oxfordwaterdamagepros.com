What this business is

Oxford Water Damage Pros is a 24/7 water damage restoration service for Oxford, Mississippi and nearby Lafayette County towns. People call the dispatch line when water is already on the floor. The goal of that call is to stop the loss from growing, get licensed and insured restoration professionals to the property, and explain the scope and price before work starts.

We keep the public side of the business simple on purpose. You will not find invented review counts, award badges or named technicians on this site, because none of those would be true. What you will find is plain information about how water damage works, what each kind of job involves and what to ask any company before you sign.

The standard we point to

The published industry standard for water damage restoration is ANSI/IICRC S500. It describes water categories, drying goals and documentation practices. We encourage homeowners to ask any restoration company whether its crews work to that standard, and to ask what moisture readings they will record. A company that answers clearly is easier to trust than one that does not.

What we do

- Emergency water extraction and structural drying

- Cleanup and repair after burst pipes, appliance leaks, storm water and flooding

- Documentation for insurance claims, including photos, readings and itemized scopes

- Rebuild work once the structure is dry, covered under each service page

What we do not do

We do not handle fire damage, mold remediation or foundation waterproofing. Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps entering your basement after every heavy rain, the long-term fix belongs to a waterproofing or drainage contractor, and we will say so on the phone. We also do not repair plumbing lines or replace water heaters, although we work alongside the plumber who does.
