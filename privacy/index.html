Privacy Policy

What information we receive, how it is used and how to ask questions.

Information we receive

Oxford Water Damage Pros is a call-only service. This website has no forms. The information we receive comes from three places. When you call (662) 757-1011, we receive your phone number and whatever details you give about the property and the damage. When you email contact@oxfordwaterdamagepros.com, we receive your email address and your message.

When you visit the site, the hosting provider records basic technical information such as the pages requested, the browser type and the approximate time of the visit.

Call recording notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call and ask how else to proceed.

How we use information

We use call details to understand the damage, give safe instructions, arrange a crew and prepare a scope of work. We use email to answer your message. We use technical visit information to keep the site working and to understand which pages are read.

Sharing

Details you give may be shared with the restoration professionals assigned to your job so they can plan the work. We also share information when the law requires it. We do not share information for any other reason without telling you first.

Cookies and third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps. Those providers may receive technical information about your visit under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and correcting information

We keep call and job records as long as needed to finish the work, answer questions about it and meet legal and tax duties. To ask us to correct or delete information you gave us, email contact@oxfordwaterdamagepros.com with the phone number you called from.

Children

This site is for adults who own or manage property. It is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version was published on 2026-11-10. Questions go to contact@oxfordwaterdamagepros.com or to the business address, 1910 University Avenue, Oxford, MS 38655.
